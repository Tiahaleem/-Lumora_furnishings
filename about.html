Our Story

It started with a simple idea

LUMORA started with a simple frustration: too much furniture forces you to choose between looking good and feeling good. We wanted pieces that didn't ask you to pick a side.

What began as a small workshop has grown into a team of designers and craftspeople who share one belief — furniture should be striking enough to notice and comfortable enough to actually live with.

We still build with sustainably sourced solid wood, natural materials, and joinery techniques that don't cut corners. Every piece is still made by hand, and we still believe the best furniture is the kind you keep for years, not seasons.

Our Philosophy

"Good furniture should be beautiful enough to notice and comfortable enough to live with."

Our Craft

From Idea to Your Home

Every piece passes through six stages, each one essential to the quality we're known for.

01

Inspiration

Every piece starts with a feeling, not a trend — how a room should work, not just how it should look.

02

Design

Ideas become sketches, then models. We test proportions for comfort and durability before a single piece of wood is cut.

03

Material Selection

We choose solid wood for grain, strength, and character — no veneers, no shortcuts.

04

Craftsmanship

Our woodworkers shape, join, and finish every piece by hand, using techniques that take years to learn.

05

Quality Check

Every piece is checked for joints, finish, and comfort before it ever leaves the workshop.

06

Your Home

It arrives ready to become part of daily life — built to last, not just to arrive looking good.
